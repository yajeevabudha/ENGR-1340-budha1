The adding of two integer happen when you combine their values while paying attention to their signs.

A example of this is when you two bananas you get one more banana that equals 3 bananas in total which is the same as 2+1 = 3.
Multiplication is repeated addition.

For example:

3 × 4 = 12

This means adding 3 four times:

3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12
